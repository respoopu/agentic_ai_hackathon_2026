Multi-Agent Architecture

Five pipeline agents, one scheduled agent off the request path, a detached validation layer, and deterministic Intake/Setup. Two stores, two capped request loops, one ledger-bounded feedback cycle.

v2.2 contract checks

The structural checks that keep the picture synchronized. Full 23-item list in architecture.md §13.

  1. 1
    Intake and gates are distinct. Deterministic I0 enforces ages 13–17; G1–G4 validate inter-agent payloads, including Broker → Observer.
  2. 2
    Every writer and reader is visible. Intake/Setup and the build-time seed loader own initial writes; Guardian reads both stores; Compliance writes freshness and dead-listing flags.
  3. 3
    No replacement bypasses safety. Booking failures and dead listings wake Planner, then the replacement traverses G2 and G3 again.
  4. 4
    Bounds match their semantics. Two request loops have hard MAX_* caps; the longitudinal cycle is bounded by finite tries_total.
  5. 5
    PoC scope is honest. Text-only debrief, sandboxed Broker, simulated cohort buckets and a persistent checkpointer.
