Multi-Agent Architecture

Five pipeline agents, one scheduled agent off the request path, and a detached validation layer. Two data stores. Three loops, every one of them capped by a counter held in state.

What changed from the v1 diagram

The five structural deltas. Full list of nineteen in architecture.md §13.

  1. 1
    Every loop has a cap. MAX_REPLANS = 3, MAX_DISCOVERY_ROUNDS = 2, MAX_GUARDIAN_REJECTIONS = 2 — held in state, not left to the model's judgement. v1 said the Planner "replans until a gate passes."
  2. 2
    The budget ledger has an owner. Money, hours and tries are spent down and reallocated rather than matched once. Planner reads, Broker decrements, Observer reconciles.
  3. 3
    A did_not_attend edge. v1 only reached the Observer along "teen attends session", which discarded the most informative event in the system.
  4. 4
    Guardian's two checks are separated. Per-listing vetting and per-plan spend approval have different granularities and different failure modes; collapsing them meant one silently didn't happen.
  5. 5
    The age boundary is enforced. 13–17 at both ends. A declared age under 13 is refused before any planning, with a referral rather than a partial plan.
